// About Me

Passionate About Planet & People

With over two decades of hands-on expertise in environmental science and international project management, I have dedicated my career to protecting and restoring the precious ecosystems of the Western Balkans. From monitoring cyanobacteria in the waters of Prespa Lake for my M.Sc. thesis, to leading multi-country climate resilience programmes as a UNDP National Project Manager — my journey has always been guided by the conviction that science must serve society.

My work bridges rigorous ecological research with real-world policy impact, engaging governments, communities, and international institutions to create lasting, measurable change. Whether designing integrated watershed management plans, building institutional capacity, or coordinating transboundary biodiversity conservation, I bring both scientific depth and strategic vision to every challenge.
